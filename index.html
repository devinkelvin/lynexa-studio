Frequently asked

Clarity before commitment.

Why 7 weeks? Is that enough to build a real product?+

Yes. 7 weeks is the sweet spot for a pre-seed/seed MVP. We spend Week 1 discovering and Week 2 designing, which cuts out scope creep. Weeks 3–6 are dedicated to parallel build tracks where our senior team develops the live MVP, brand assets, site, and deck simultaneously. Week 7 is scale preparation. This rapid speed is only possible because we have zero junior developers and zero handoff lag.

What frontend and backend technology stack do you build on?+

We default to modern, scalable industry standards. For web MVPs, we use React and Next.js. For mobile apps, we build native-cross-platform using Flutter or React Native. Our backends are engineered with Node.js, Python, or Go, and deployed securely to AWS or Google Cloud. We integrate fintech rails (Paystack, Flutterwave, Mono, Okra) and security verification protocols (BVN/NIN, NIBSS) natively.

Who owns the IP and codebase after handoff?+

You do. 100%. Upon final engagement delivery, we hand over clean GitHub repositories, design tokens, raw Figma kits, and deck files. The intellectual property is fully transferred to your company. We also provide handover documentation so any incoming CTO or developer team can step in immediately without friction.

Do we sign an NDA before sharing ideas?+

Absolutely. We respect founder confidentiality. Before our initial deep-dive or scoping calls, we are happy to sign a standard mutual NDA to protect your proprietary ideas and market insights.

Do you help with fundraising and investor pitching?+

Yes, it is built directly into our full method. We don’t just build the product; we design the visual layer of your financial models, craft your investor deck, and assist in coaching your narrative presentation. We build the story alongside the software.
